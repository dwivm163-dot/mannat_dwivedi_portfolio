OTHER PROJECTS 002

Past Life Archive — Digital Divination System

A CC final project that turns divination toward the past: users enter birth details and receive a generated “past life” record built from Wikipedia’s On This Day API layered with fictional interpretation—half archive, half ritual. Built as a standalone site and hosted on GitHub Pages from the cc-final repository.

reflection

Design Process & Reflection

For this project, I wanted to explore divination from a different perspective by focusing on the past rather than the future. Instead of creating a typical fortune-telling experience, I was more interested in how systems can construct meaning from fragments of historical data and personal information. Using Wikipedia’s “On This Day” API allowed me to combine real historical records with fictional interpretation, creating an experience that feels both believable and slightly uncanny.

One of the most important parts of the project for me was the interface design. I chose a vintage archival aesthetic inspired by old documents, records, and filing systems because I wanted the experience to feel like retrieving a forgotten identity rather than taking a personality quiz. I also liked the contrast between spiritual ideas such as reincarnation and the cold, bureaucratic structure of an archive system.

Technically, the project helped me become more comfortable working with APIs and structuring data through JavaScript. If I were to continue developing the project, I would expand the system by adding more varied historical mappings, multiple archive outcomes, and deeper personalization based on birthplace or birth time to make each generated record feel even more unique.
